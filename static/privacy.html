Privacy and data

Return to AYO!

Your location

Location is requested when you choose a nearby-location feature. Coordinates are sent to this server to find stops. They are not stored in the community database or favorites. Production access logs are configured to omit query strings and peer IP addresses. Hosting providers may have their own logs.

Favorites and language

Favorites, theme and language preferences stay in this browser's local storage. Clearing browser data removes them. No account or cross-device sync is provided.

Winter Watch

Reports are anonymous to other riders, text-only, unverified and moderated before publication. Do not include names, contact details, identifying information or links. Approved reports disappear from public results after 24 hours. Operators must run retention cleanup to delete reports more than seven days past expiry and moderator audit records older than 30 days. Retained backups require separate protection and deletion. Voting uses a random browser cookie; clearing cookies can bypass per-browser deduplication. Daily keyed request identifiers support short-lived abuse limits. These controls do not establish a rider's identity.

Other providers

Map tiles and weather contact third-party providers and can reveal your IP address. The map library and fonts are self-hosted. External directions links pass selected stop coordinates to another provider. Browser speech and notification support depend on your device.

Background arrival watches are opt-in, one-shot and expire after 24 hours. Push subscription addresses, keys and selected stops are encrypted in the database. Stop background alerts to remove your watches. Notifications may appear on a device lock screen. Your browser or push provider controls delivery; it is not guaranteed.

Offline

The service worker caches the website shell, not live transit APIs. Offline pages cannot establish current bus locations, departures, conditions or service disruptions.

Questions about data handling should go to the site's published operator contact before using the public deployment. Hosting, retention ownership and a support contact must be configured before launch.

Resumen en español

Tu ubicación se solicita al usar funciones cercanas. Se envía al servidor para buscar paradas y no se guarda con favoritos ni informes. Los favoritos y preferencias se guardan en este navegador. Los informes públicos son anónimos, no verificados y revisados antes de publicarse; caducan en 24 horas. No incluyas datos personales. Los mapas, fuentes, clima y enlaces externos pueden contactar a otros proveedores. Sin conexión no hay datos de transporte actuales.
