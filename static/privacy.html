Privacy and data

Return to AYO!

Your location

Location is requested when you choose a nearby-location feature. Coordinates are sent to this server to find stops. They are not stored in the community database or favorites. Production access logs are configured to omit query strings and peer IP addresses. Hosting providers may have their own logs.

Favorites and language

Favorites, theme and language preferences stay in this browser's local storage. Clearing browser data removes them. No account or cross-device sync is provided.

Winter Watch

Reports are anonymous to other riders, text-only, unverified and moderated before publication. Do not include names, contact details, identifying information or links. Approved reports disappear from public results after 24 hours. Operators must run retention cleanup to delete reports more than seven days past expiry and moderator audit records older than 30 days. Retained backups require separate protection and deletion. Voting uses a random browser cookie; clearing cookies can bypass per-browser deduplication. Daily keyed request identifiers support short-lived abuse limits. These controls do not establish a rider's identity.

Other providers

Map tiles and weather contact third-party providers and can reveal your IP address. The map library and fonts are self-hosted. External directions links pass selected stop coordinates to another provider. Browser speech and notification support depend on your device.

Background arrival watches are opt-in, one-shot and expire after 24 hours. Push subscription addresses, keys and selected stops are encrypted in the database. Stop background alerts to remove your watches. Notifications may appear on a device lock screen. Your browser or push provider controls delivery; it is not guaranteed.

Offline

The service worker caches the website shell, not live transit APIs. Offline pages cannot establish current bus locations, departures, conditions or service disruptions.

Questions about data handling should go to the site's published operator contact before using the public deployment. Hosting, retention ownership and a support contact must be configured before launch.

Resumen en español

Tu ubicación se solicita al usar funciones cercanas. Se envía al servidor para buscar paradas y no se guarda con favoritos ni informes. Los favoritos y preferencias se guardan en este navegador. Los informes públicos son anónimos, no verificados y revisados antes de publicarse; caducan en 24 horas. No incluyas datos personales. Los mapas, fuentes, clima y enlaces externos pueden contactar a otros proveedores. Sin conexión no hay datos de transporte actuales.

AYO conversation and microphone

The typed transit helper sends only your request and explicitly selected transit context to this site's validated tools. It is a supported-request resolver when paid AI is not connected, not a language model. Typed conversation history stays in the page and is cleared when the workspace is rebuilt. Microphone access is requested only after Start Conversation and only when the operator has configured live AI. When enabled, microphone audio and conversation/tool context are processed by OpenAI Realtime through WebRTC. This application does not record or store audio or transcripts on its server. The provider's retention and processing terms require operator review; this statement does not promise zero provider retention. Stop, close, hide the page or disconnect to stop local media capture. Native device and provider delivery require acceptance testing.

Live paid voice is disabled by default and blocked without API credentials/billing, a trusted server-side authentication adapter and approved spending safeguards. Daily global/account admission counters store a hashed account identifier; maintenance can remove counters older than two days. Browser duration limits are not a tamper-proof billing cap: production requires provider-level spending controls and server-side session supervision before approval. No private API key is sent to this browser.

When changing languages, selected public transit IDs and the active route can be kept once in tab session storage, revalidated against GTFS after reload and then removed. Raw GPS coordinates, audio and conversation transcripts are not stored for this handoff.

Conversación y micrófono

La ayuda escrita envía la solicitud y el contexto de transporte elegido a herramientas validadas de este sitio. Sin IA conectada resuelve solicitudes admitidas; no es un modelo de lenguaje. El historial queda en la página y se borra al reconstruir el espacio. El micrófono solo se solicita al iniciar una conversación con IA configurada. Si se activa, OpenAI Realtime procesa el audio y contexto mediante WebRTC. Esta aplicación no graba ni guarda audio o transcripciones en su servidor. Las condiciones de conservación del proveedor requieren revisión; no se promete conservación cero. Detener, cerrar, ocultar la página o desconectarse detiene la captura local.

La voz de pago está desactivada por defecto y requiere credenciales, facturación, autenticación del servidor, controles de gasto y pruebas reales. Los límites del navegador no garantizan un límite de facturación. Los contadores de admisión guardan un identificador de cuenta seudónimo obtenido mediante hash; el mantenimiento puede eliminar los de más de dos días. Las claves privadas nunca se envían al navegador.

Al cambiar de idioma se pueden conservar una vez los ID de transporte y la ruta activa en el almacenamiento de la pestaña. Se validan con GTFS al recargar y se eliminan. No se guardan coordenadas GPS, audio ni transcripciones para este cambio.
